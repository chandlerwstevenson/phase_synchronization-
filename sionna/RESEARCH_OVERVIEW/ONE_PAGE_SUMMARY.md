# Distributed-Array Phase Synchronization — One-Page Technical Summary

**Problem.** N distributed transmitters achieve coherent gain G = |Σaᵢe^{jθᵢ}|²/(Σaᵢ)² only while per-station carrier phases stay locked; the two-way exchanges that maintain lock (half-difference ½·wrap(φ_fwd−φ_rev) cancels the reciprocal channel) consume shared airtime that scales with N. All results below are waveform-level simulation (Sionna, 915 MHz, full oscillator/RF impairments, datasheet ADEV-anchored oscillator classes), multi-seed, with pre-registered predictions and discriminating controls.

**Error floor and cadence law.** Steady residual decomposes as σ² ≈ q_θ + (σ_ω⁺LT)² + σ_track² (drift + latency-coupled frequency posterior + tracking; Riccati-computable ex ante). Setting σ(τ) = budget yields a per-station coast time; the covariance-cycle fixed point P* = update(predictᵐ(P*)) predicted **99.5% of ~26,000 individual service gaps exactly, zero fitted constants**, N-independent. A six-term per-exchange floor budget (thermal, intra-capture walk σ_pn√(L/3), clock-offset fractional resampling of delay-separated multipath [10–25 mrad, conditionally white], turnaround×Doppler nonreciprocity bias π·cos(π/4)f_Dδt [≤2% prediction error; structurally invisible to CFO-based correction], oscillator excess) verified additive to 0.89–1.00. *Retracted en route (by our own frozen-oscillator controls): a "dominant multipath resampling floor" (~100 mrad was class-proportional unmodeled oscillator noise), an interior-optimal observation rate (acquisition transient), a Doppler coast term (seed noise) — corrections preserved in-place.*

**In-band ("opportunistic") synchronization.** One-way receptions of ordinary data/sensing bursts observe θᵢ−θⱼ+ψᵢⱼ. We proved the θ/ψ split is a gauge: the observability Gramian is rank-deficient with null direction exactly (1,0,−1) — no volume of one-way data separates clock from channel; sparse two-way exchanges restore rank, with identifiability iff the two-way subgraph spans (later found isomorphic to Freris–Graham–Kumar, IEEE TAC 2011, in delay vocabulary — cited, not claimed). A 3-state filter fusing free OFDM observations (4.4–5.2 mrad/observation across 11 environments incl. ray-traced no-LOS urban; capture-length model shows this is geometry, not waveform) with sparse two-way exchanges holds ~55 mrad at **0.5% sync airtime vs 87 mrad at 19.1%** conventional, scales to N=30 where conventional saturates (N≈16), bounded by environment coherence time (three regimes; boundary πf_D T K = b, validated within one grid step). Proved + measured: the "sync-less-often-is-better" reversal exists *only* in this architecture (exchanges become net noise injection); no dedicated-only loop reverses. The mod-π branch ambiguity (half-difference observes wrap(2θ); anti-phase is a zero-innovation attractor — 10/20 adverse acquisitions capture silently) is resolved by exactly one bit with a derived periodic-check rate.

**Topology × protocol (current lead result).** Selecting sync links is conventionally an estimation problem (BLUE variance = effective resistance; Barooah–Hespanha, Karp). Three findings: (1) **min-MSE ≠ max-gain** — expected gain is an *amplitude-weighted* Kirchhoff index; heterogeneous amplitudes diverge in 16/18 exact-enumerated configs, costing ≤8.6 gain points, incl. a provable-indifference star instance (+3.6 pts); (2) **joint node+edge selection**: partial participation wins in a budget *window* (at 5%: all 12 instances bench 3/8 radios, ≤78% relative gain); (3) **the pre-registered three-protocol fork (118 cells)**: topology stability has three mechanisms of three origins — *physical* (cadence ceiling m≲2 from frequency walk; survives all protocols), *numerical* (degree ceiling; Jacobi divergence, spectral radius 1.107 — cured by direction/damping at 92%, *not* by sequencing, which re-enters the cadence mechanism), *topological* (cycle penalty; settled states carry **exactly quantized winding numbers**, cycle sums = 2π·w ±0.001 — robust to any local update rule, per the cut-space structure; measured independently by three agents). Headline: **topology ranking inverts across protocols** (star: 23.7% simultaneous → 99.4% directed; at N=16 all bidirectional protocols collapse, directed holds 89–99%) — topology and protocol are not separable design choices. Dynamic re-selection under 20 dB blockage: a no-oracle link-quality trigger captures 95% of oracle (0.938 vs 0.683 static), zero false switches; pays only for episodes ≥~50 intervals.
